### 摘要

摘 要：以科技创新为核心动力的新质生产力是推动区域经济实现高质量发展的关键引擎。基于投资结构优化与全要素生产率提高的内在理论逻辑，本文系统分析了新质生产力赋能无锡经济增长的作用机理与具体路径。研究结果表明，无锡市在产业升级投资、绿色技术投资以及数字新基建投资三大领域已取得阶段性成效，但也面临着产业结构失衡问题突出、绿色转型成本压力较大、数字基础设施供需脱节等现实困境。基于此，本文提出产业升级投资需聚焦存量提质与增量布局协同推进、绿色技术投资要降低转型成本与增强内生动力、数字新基建投资则应探索化解技术迭代风险与转化应用瓶颈等针对性投资路径，以期为无锡市优化投资结构、释放新质生产力活力、推动经济高质量发展提供理论支持与实践参考。
Abstract: New quality productive forces, driven by scientific and technological innovation as the core impetus, are a key engine for promoting high-quality regional economic development. Based on the internal theoretical logic of optimizing investment structure and improving total factor productivity, this paper systematically analyzes the mechanism and pathways through which new quality productive forces empower economic growth in Wuxi. The results show that Wuxi has achieved phased progress in three major areas, including industrial upgrading investment, green technology investment, and digital new infrastructure investment. However, it still faces practical challenges such as prominent industrial structure imbalance, significant cost pressures in green transformation, and mismatches between supply and demand in digital infrastructure. Based on these findings, this paper proposes targeted investment pathways: industrial upgrading investment should focus on coordinated advancement of stock optimization and incremental allocation; green technology investment should aim to reduce transformation costs and enhance endogenous momentum; and digital new infrastructure investment should address technological iteration risks and application bottlenecks. The study aims to provide theoretical support and practical references for optimizing investment structure, releasing the vitality of new quality productive forces, and promoting high-quality economic development in Wuxi.
